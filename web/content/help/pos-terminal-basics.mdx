The Terminal is Ascend's register screen — built for fast, barcode-driven
checkout.

## Adding items to a cart

Scan a barcode, or search and tap a product in the grid. Age-restricted
products require an age verification checkbox before checkout is allowed.

## Discounts

Tap **Discount** in the action bar to apply a cart-level discount. The
discount is saved to the order as you go, the same as every other cart
change — nothing is calculated only at the very end.

## Taking payment

Tap **Complete sale** once the cart has at least one item and any required
age verification is confirmed. The tender screen supports cash, card, and
(where enabled) split payments across multiple methods.

## Working offline

If the network drops mid-sale, the Terminal queues your changes locally and
keeps working. An offline banner appears so you always know your connection
state. Once the connection returns, queued sales sync automatically — you
don't need to redo anything.

## After the sale

The receipt screen shows the completed sale and starts a new one when you're
ready for the next customer. Need to look up a past sale, issue a return, or
void something? See [Sales history](/help/sales-history-and-returns).
