## KPIs

The top of the dashboard shows revenue, sale count, gross profit, customer
count, and average sale value for the selected date range, updating live as
new sales and payments come in.

## What to do next

The recommendations list is rule-based, not a guess — every item is derived
from a real signal in your data: products missing cost prices, low or
out-of-stock inventory, thin gross margin, uncategorized expenses, and more.
Each one links directly to where you'd act on it, and you can turn any of
them into a tracked task.

## AI briefing

If your plan includes the AI Assistant, you may see a short narrated summary
above the recommendations list — a plain-language read of the same
rule-based data above it, not a separate analysis. It never invents a
number, product, or fact that isn't already in your recommendations; if
narration isn't available for any reason, the rule-based list underneath is
unaffected and still fully accurate on its own.

## Quick actions

The quick actions section is a shortcut to the most common next steps —
creating a purchase order, receiving stock, ringing up a sale — without
navigating through the sidebar first.
