## Why receiving matters

Every stock change in Ascend is recorded as an immutable inventory movement —
receiving, sales, transfers, and adjustments all leave a permanent trail.
This is what makes reports, reorder alerts, and stock levels trustworthy: a
quantity on screen is always backed by a real history of how it got there.

## Recording a receipt

From **Inventory → Receive**, select the product and enter the quantity and
cost. If your business tracks lots or expiry dates, you'll be asked for that
at the same time — it's recorded against this specific receipt, not the
product in general, so mixed-expiry stock stays traceable.

## Reorder alerts

Once a product's on-hand quantity drops to or below its reorder point,
it surfaces as a reorder alert on the dashboard and in **Purchasing**. If a
supplier has a minimum order quantity or case pack size on file, the
suggested reorder quantity automatically rounds up to a valid order size —
you won't see a suggestion to order a fraction of a case.

## Transfers between locations

If you operate multiple outlets, **Inventory → Transfers** moves stock
between them as a single atomic operation — both legs succeed together or
neither does, so a failed transfer can never leave stock "in transit" and
unaccounted for.
