Ascend is one platform with configurable business packs — retail, wholesale,
restaurant, and more all share the same underlying product, inventory, order,
and payment engine. Retail is the first fully complete pack, so it's the
default starting point for new accounts.

## 1. Create your account

Sign up with your business email. This creates your tenant (your isolated
business account) and makes you the owner. Owners have full access to every
enabled module and can invite additional team members later from **Team**.

## 2. Choose a business type

During setup you'll pick a business type — retail, wholesale, restaurant, and
so on. This installs a sensible default: which modules are enabled, what
navigation you see, and which fields are required on products and customers.
You can change this later from **Settings → Business Modes**, and Ascend will
show you exactly what changes before you confirm the switch.

## 3. Finish the setup checklist

The dashboard shows a setup checklist for anything still missing before you
can start selling:

- Add your first outlet and register
- Set up tax rates and payment methods
- Add your first product
- Receive initial stock

The checklist disappears once everything is complete — it won't nag you
about steps that don't apply to your business type.

## 4. Make your first sale

Once setup is complete, head to **Terminal** to ring up a sale, or **Orders**
if you're running a wholesale/invoice-first business. See
[POS Terminal basics](/help/pos-terminal-basics) for a walkthrough of the
register.
